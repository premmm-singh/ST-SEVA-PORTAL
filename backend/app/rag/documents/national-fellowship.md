# National Fellowship for Higher Education of ST Students (NFST)
Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), Government of India

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objectives
The National Fellowship for Higher Education of ST Students (formerly Rajiv Gandhi National Fellowship for ST) provides fellowships to Scheduled Tribe students to pursue higher studies such as M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees in Sciences, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Engineering/Technology at UGC-recognized Indian universities and institutions.

## 2. Annual Slots & Quotas
- **Total Annual Fresh Slots**: **750 slots per year**.
- **Gender Reservation**: 33% of the slots are reserved for ST female candidates.
- **Divyangjan Reservation**: 5% of the slots are reserved for ST candidates with disabilities.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. Must belong to a recognized Scheduled Tribe.
2. Must have completed Master's degree examination or equivalent with minimum 50% marks for ST candidates.
3. Must be registered/admitted for regular and full-time M.Phil./Ph.D. in a University/Institute recognized by UGC.
4. **No Family Income Ceiling**: Unlike pre-matric and post-matric schemes, there is **no family income ceiling** for the National Fellowship (NFST). Selection is strictly merit-based.
5. Candidate cannot hold dual fellowship (e.g. CSIR-JRF, ICMR, or ICAR concurrently).

## 4. Financial Assistance Rates (Revised UGC Benchmarks)
Fellowships align directly with UGC Junior Research Fellow (JRF) and Senior Research Fellow (SRF) pay bands:

| Fellowship Stage | Monthly Stipend | Contingency (Humanities & Social Sciences) | Contingency (Sciences, Engg, Tech) | House Rent Allowance (HRA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JRF (Initial 2 Years)** | Rs. 37,000 per month | Rs. 10,000 per annum | Rs. 12,000 per annum | As per Govt rules (8%, 16%, or 24% of fellowship depending on city X/Y/Z) |
| **SRF (Remaining 3 Years)**| Rs. 42,000 per month | Rs. 20,500 per annum | Rs. 25,000 per annum | As per Govt rules (8%, 16%, or 24% of fellowship depending on city X/Y/Z) |

### Escort / Reader Allowance:
- For physically handicapped / visually impaired candidates: Rs. 2,000 per month.

## 5. Tenure of Fellowship
- M.Phil: Maximum 2 Years.
- M.Phil + Ph.D: Maximum 5 Years (2 Years M.Phil JRF + 3 Years Ph.D SRF).
- Ph.D: Maximum 5 Years (2 Years JRF + 3 Years SRF, upgraded after 2-year performance review by three-member committee).
