## 2. Common Ineligibility Restrictions
1. **Holding Dual Scholarships**: Availing concurrent scholarships from UGC, CSIR, ICMR, AICTE, or any Central/State scheme for the same course will lead to immediate cancellation and recovery.
2. **Repeating a Class**: Students repeating the same standard or academic year due to failure are ineligible for maintenance allowance for that repeated year.
3. **Lateral or Downward Course Shifts**: Students switching to an equivalent or lower qualification (e.g. B.Com after completing B.A.) are not eligible.
4. **Part-time / Private Candidates**: Enrolled as casual or private candidates without regular classroom attendance are strictly excluded.
5. **Distance Education Exception**: In distance/correspondence mode, only compulsory non-refundable fees are reimbursed; maintenance allowance is not provided.
