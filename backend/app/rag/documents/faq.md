# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - ST Scholarships & Portal Services
Ministry of Tribal Affairs Knowledge Base

### Q1: What is the maximum family income limit for the Post-Matric ST Scholarship?
**A**: The family income ceiling is **Rs. 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees per annum)** from all combined sources.

### Q2: Is there any income limit for the National Fellowship for ST (NFST)?
**A**: **No**, there is no family income limit for the National Fellowship for Higher Education of ST Students (NFST). Selection is purely merit-based for M.Phil and Ph.D. scholars.

### Q3: What is the income limit for the Top Class Education Scheme?
**A**: The annual family income ceiling is **Rs. 6,00,000 (Six Lakh Rupees)** for Top Class Scholarship in 265 notified premier institutions.

### Q4: Can I apply for both Post-Matric Scholarship and a State Government Scholarship?
**A**: **No**. Holding two government scholarships concurrently for the same course is strictly prohibited. If detected, both will be cancelled and recovery initiated.

### Q5: How much is the maintenance allowance for Post-Matric hostellers in Medical or Engineering courses?
**A**: Hostellers in Group 1 courses (Engineering, Medicine, Technology, Agriculture, Management) receive **Rs. 1,200 per month** for 10 months. Day scholars receive **Rs. 550 per month**.

### Q6: What benefits does the Top Class Scheme provide for computer purchase?
**A**: ST scholars in notified premier institutes receive a **one-time grant of Rs. 45,000** for purchasing a laptop or desktop computer with accessories.

### Q7: What are the monthly rates for National Fellowship (NFST)?
**A**: Junior Research Fellow (JRF): **Rs. 37,000 per month**. Senior Research Fellow (SRF): **Rs. 42,000 per month**, plus HRA (8%-24%) and annual contingency grants of Rs. 10,000 - 25,000.

### Q8: What should I do if my application status shows "Defective"?
**A**: If marked "Defective", log in to the portal, read the defect remark left by your Institute Nodal Officer, rectify the error (e.g. re-upload correct marksheet or fee receipt), and re-submit within 10 days.

### Q9: Why was my DBT scholarship payment rejected with "NPCI Inactive / Aadhaar Not Seeded"?
**A**: Your bank account is not mapped to your Aadhaar on the NPCI National Payments gateway. Visit your home bank branch immediately and submit the "Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI Mapper Consent Form" or link via mobile banking app.

### Q10: How many slots are available in the National Overseas Scholarship (NOS)?
**A**: There are **20 slots per year** for ST candidates to pursue Masters or Ph.D. abroad at universities ranked in the Top 1000 QS World Rankings.

### Q11: How long is a Caste Certificate valid?
**A**: An official Scheduled Tribe caste certificate issued by an authorized revenue authority has **lifetime validity**, unless revoked by a state scrutiny committee.

### Q12: How long is an Income Certificate valid?
**A**: An income certificate is valid for **one financial year** (from 1st April to 31st March) and must be renewed every academic year for scholarship renewal.

### Q13: What is the toll-free national helpline for ST scholarships?
**A**: You can call the Ministry of Tribal Affairs Toll-Free Helpline at **1800-11-7777** or NSP Helpline at **0120-6619540** (available 8 AM to 8 PM on working days).

### Q14: How can I track my scholarship payment status?
**A**: You can track payment status via the "DBT Tracker" tab on the ST Seva Portal or on PFMS portal (`pfms.nic.in`) using "Track NSP Payments" with your Bank Account Number or Application ID.
