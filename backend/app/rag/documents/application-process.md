# Step-by-Step ST Scholarship Application & Verification Process
National Scholarship Portal (NSP) & ST Seva Portal Workflow

## 1. Step 1: One-Time Registration (OTR)
- Visit `https://scholarships.gov.in` or the ST Seva Portal mobile application.
- Complete Aadhaar Face Authentication or OTP authentication on your registered mobile number.
- Generate your permanent 14-digit OTR Reference Number.

## 2. Step 2: Online Application Submission
1. Log in with OTR credentials.
2. Select Scheme: "Ministry of Tribal Affairs" -> Choose the applicable scheme (Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, or Top Class).
3. Fill Academic Details: Institute AISHE code, admission roll number, course, and year.
4. Auto-Fetch Documents via DigiLocker: Fetch verified Caste, Income, and Domicile certificates.
5. Enter Bank Details: Ensure the bank account is active and seeded with Aadhaar on NPCI mapper.
6. Final Submit and print the Application Acknowledgement Slip with tracking ID.

## 3. Step 3: Multi-Level Institutional & Government Scrutiny
- **Level 1 (L1) - Institute Nodal Officer (INO) Scrutiny**:
  - Verification of bonafide admission, course fee structure, and attendance (minimum 75%).
  - Forwarded to District Welfare Office within 15 working days.
- **Level 2 (L2) - District Welfare Officer (DWO) Scrutiny**:
  - Validation of Caste certificate authenticity with State revenue databases.
  - Cross-check of family income limit (<= Rs 2.5L or 6L).
  - Verification of residential domicile.
- **Level 3 (L3) - State Nodal Officer (SNO) Approval & Sanction**:
  - Aggregation of approved district lists.
  - Generation of state merit list and approval of state share (25% / 10%).

## 4. Step 4: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Disbursal Pipeline
- **PFMS Validation**: Public Financial Management System verifies bank IFSC and account status.
- **NPCI Aadhaar Mapping**: Payment dispatched via Aadhaar Payment Bridge System (APBS).
- **SMS & WhatsApp Notification**: Automatic SMS and WhatsApp alert triggered upon successful fund credit.
- **Timeline**: Funds are typically credited within 21 to 30 days of final SNO sanction order.
