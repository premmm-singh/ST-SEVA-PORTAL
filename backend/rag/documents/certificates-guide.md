# Guide to Mandatory Certificates for ST Scholarships
Verification, Issuing Authorities, Digital Signatures & DigiLocker Standards

## 1. Caste / Community Certificate (Scheduled Tribe)
- **Purpose**: Establishes constitutional status under Article 342 as an ST member in the applicant's home State/UT.
- **Competent Issuing Authorities**:
  1. District Magistrate / Additional District Magistrate / Collector / Deputy Commissioner / Deputy Collector / 1st Class Stipendiary Magistrate / Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) / Taluka Magistrate / Executive Magistrate.
  2. Chief Presidency Magistrate / Additional Chief Presidency Magistrate / Presidency Magistrate.
  3. Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tehsildar.
  4. Sub-Divisional Officer of the area where the candidate and/or family normally resides.
- **Validity**: **Lifetime validity**, unless formally revoked or cancelled by the State Scrutiny Committee.
- **Digital Verification**: Must feature a 2D QR barcode, digital signature (DSC), or JharSewa / e-District / MeeSeva registration number verifiable via API.

## 2. Family Income Certificate
- **Purpose**: Verifies that the family's total gross income falls within the scheme's statutory ceiling.
- **Competent Issuing Authorities**: Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Circle Officer (CO), or Block Development Officer (BDO). Affidavits executed on stamp paper without revenue officer attestation are **strictly not accepted**.
- **Validity Period**: Valid for **one financial year** (from 1st April of current year to 31st March of the following year). Must be renewed annually for scholarship renewals.
- **Inclusions**: Salaries, agricultural income, business proceeds, pensions, and remittances of all earning family members.

## 3. Domicile / Residential Certificate
- **Purpose**: Confirms the candidate's permanent state residency to ensure state allocation quotas and state share contribution.
- **Issuing Authority**: Circle Officer (CO), Tehsildar, or Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO).
- **Validity**: Permanent or as mandated by the respective State Government's Citizen Services Charter.

## 4. Institutional Bonafide & Fee Structure Certificate
- **Purpose**: Verifies active, regular full-time enrollment in an approved institution and course.
- **Key Details Required**:
  - Full Name of Student & Enrollment / Roll Number.
  - Course Name, Branch, and Current Year / Semester.
  - Institution AISHE (All India Survey on Higher Education) Code.
  - Itemized Breakup of Tuition Fees, Examination Fees, Library Fees, and Hostel Charges.
- **Issuing Authority**: Head of the Institution / Dean / Principal / Registrar with official seal and signature.

## 5. DigiLocker Integration Workflow
- All official certificates issued by State e-District portals are fetchable into the ST Seva Portal via DigiLocker URI without manual scanning.
- Certificates fetched via DigiLocker possess legal validity under Section 7A of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
