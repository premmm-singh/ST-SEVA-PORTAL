# Annual Deadlines Calendar & Disbursement Timelines
Ministry of Tribal Affairs Academic Year Schedule

## 1. Annual Scheme Cycle Timeline

| Phase / Milestone | Typical Start Date | Final Closing Deadline | Applicable Schemes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NSP Portal Open for Fresh & Renewal Applications** | 1st July | 30th November | Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Top Class |
| **National Fellowship (NFST) Application Window** | 1st August | 15th October | NFST (M.Phil / Ph.D.) |
| **National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) Window** | 15th February | 31st March | NOS (Foreign Universities) |
| **Level 1 (Institute Verification) Deadline** | Continuous | 15th December | Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Top Class |
| **Level 2 (District Welfare Officer) Deadline** | Continuous | 31st December | Pre-Matric, Post-Matric |
| **Level 3 (State Nodal Officer) Sanction** | 1st January | 31st January | All Domestic Schemes |
| **PFMS DBT Batch Disbursal Round 1** | 1st February | 28th February | All Verified Applications |
| **PFMS DBT Batch Disbursal Round 2 (Mop-up)** | 1st March | 31st March | Corrected / Re-submitted Applications |

## 2. Renewal Window Instructions
- Renewal applications do not require full document re-upload; only updated annual income certificate and previous year pass marksheet are required.
- Students who fail to renew before the closing date must apply as Fresh applicants in the subsequent academic session.

## 3. Grace Periods & Correction Windows
- Defective application correction window: 10 days from notification by the Institute Nodal Officer.
- Discrepancy resolution on rejected bank accounts: within 14 calendar days via ST Seva Portal bank update desk.
