# National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education of ST Students (Top Class Education Scheme)
Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), Government of India

## 1. Objective of Top Class Education Scheme
The Top Class Education Scheme is a 100% Central Sector Scheme designed to recognize and promote quality education amongst ST students beyond Class XII by providing full financial support for studies in 265 notified premier institutions in India (IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, NLUs, IIITs, NID, NIFT, and leading Central Universities).

## 2. Total Slots & Selection
- **Total Annual Fresh Slots**: **1,000 fresh scholarship slots** allocated annually across the 265 premier institutions.
- **Institute-Wise Cap**: Every premier institute has a notified quota of seats for ST scholars.
- **Selection Basis**: Merit of admission secured in the premier institute based on National Entrance Tests (JEE Advanced, CAT, NEET, CLAT, etc.).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Community**: Student must belong to Scheduled Tribe community.
2. **Admission Status**: Must have gained confirmed admission to a notified Top Class premier institution according to institute admission rules.
3. **Income Ceiling**: Total family income from all sources must not exceed **Rs. 6,00,000 (Rupees Six Lakh per annum)**.
4. **Gender Parity**: 30% of total slots are reserved for female ST students.
5. **Continuation Condition**: Continued eligibility in subsequent years requires maintaining satisfactory progress and passing the annual/semester examinations without backlogs.

## 4. Financial Benefits Provided
The Top Class Scholarship provides comprehensive financial coverage:

| Component | Financial Support Provided |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Full Tuition Fees** | 100% full non-refundable tuition fees and other compulsory fees charged by the institute (capped at Rs. 2.5 Lakh/yr for private institutions, no cap for government institutions). |
| **Living Expenses** | Rs. 3,000 per month (Rs. 36,000 per academic year) per student. |
| **Books and Stationery** | Rs. 5,000 per annum per student. |
| **Computer/Laptop Assistance** | One-time grant of Rs. 45,000 for purchasing a latest laptop/computer with accessories and software during the tenure of the course. |

## 5. List of Notified Institutions (Sample Categories)
- **Engineering & Technology**: IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, IIT Kanpur, IIT Roorkee, NIT Trichy, NIT Surathkal, etc.
- **Management**: IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Calcutta, IIM Lucknow, IIM Indore, IIM Kozhikode, XLRI.
- **Medicine**: AIIMS New Delhi, PGIMER Chandigarh, JIPMER Puducherry.
- **Law**: NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, WBNUJS Kolkata, NLU Delhi.
- **Design & Fashion**: NID Ahmedabad, NIFT New Delhi.
