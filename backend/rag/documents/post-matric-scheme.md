# Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for ST Students (Class XI to Post-Doctoral)
Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), Government of India

## 1. Scheme Objective & Scope
The Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for ST Students is the flagship Centrally Sponsored Scheme providing financial support to Scheduled Tribe students pursuing recognized post-secondary education (from Class XI, XII, ITI, Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, M.Phil, to Ph.D. levels).

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
1. **Community**: Candidate must belong to a Scheduled Tribe notified under Article 342 of the Constitution of India for the respective State/UT.
2. **Family Income Limit**: Total annual family income from all sources should not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees per annum)**.
3. **Course Recognition**: Enrolled in a recognized college, university, or polytechnic approved by UGC, AICTE, NCVT, MCI, DCI, or State Education Boards.
4. **Distance Learning / Correspondence**: Eligible only for reimbursement of non-refundable fees and course books.
5. **Progressive Advancement**: Scholarships are paid for studying one stage above. Students who have completed an MA/M.Sc. cannot obtain scholarship for another MA/M.Sc. or lower degree.

## 3. Financial Benefits & Maintenance Rates
The scheme provides full coverage of non-refundable compulsory fees charged by the educational institution PLUS a graded monthly maintenance allowance for 10 months annually:

| Course Group | Courses Covered | Hosteller Rate (Per Month) | Day Scholar Rate (Per Month) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group 1** | Degree & PG in Medicine, Engineering, Technology, Agriculture, Veterinary, Management, Commercial Pilot | Rs. 1,200 | Rs. 550 |
| **Group 2** | Professional Courses, Pharmacy, Nursing, LLB, B.Ed, M.Ed, Chartered Accountancy (CA), CS, ICWA | Rs. 820 | Rs. 530 |
| **Group 3** | General Undergraduate & Postgraduate Degrees (BA, B.Com, B.Sc, MA, M.Com, M.Sc) | Rs. 570 | Rs. 300 |
| **Group 4** | Class XI, XII, Intermediate, ITI, Certificate Courses (minimum 1 year duration) | Rs. 380 | Rs. 230 |

### Additional Allowances:
- Study tour charges: up to Rs. 1,600 per annum for professional courses.
- Thesis typing and printing charges: up to Rs. 1,600 for research scholars.
- Special allowances for ST students with disabilities: Rs. 240 to Rs. 380 per month.
- Compulsory non-refundable tuition fees, registration fees, library fees, and exam fees reimbursed 100%.

## 4. Disbursal Mechanism via DBT
- Funds are disbursed directly into the student's Aadhaar-linked Bank Account via the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) and National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge System (APBS).
- Sharing ratio between Central Government and State Governments is 75:25 for general states, and 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states (100% for UTs without legislature).

## 5. Required Documents Checklist
- ST Community Certificate issued by Competent Revenue Authority.
- Income Certificate valid for the current financial year.
- Previous qualifying examination marksheet (Self-attested).
- Current Year College Admission Receipt and Bonafide Certificate showing AISHE code.
- Approved College Fee Structure showing non-refundable fees.
- Aadhaar Card and linked Active Bank Passbook/Statement.
