# National Overseas Scholarship for ST Candidates (NOS)
Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), Government of India

## 1. Scheme Overview
The National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) provides financial assistance to meritorious ST students selected for pursuing Master's level courses and Ph.D. abroad in accredited foreign universities.

## 2. Annual Slots & Subject Coverage
- **Total Annual Fresh Slots**: **20 slots per year**.
  - 30% of total awards (minimum 6 slots) earmarked for women candidates.
- **University Ranking Eligibility**: Only candidates admitted to institutes ranked within the **Top 1000** in the latest QS World University Rankings are eligible.
- **Eligible Fields of Study**:
  1. Engineering and Management Sciences (6 slots)
  2. Pure and Applied Sciences (4 slots)
  3. Agricultural Sciences and Medicine (4 slots)
  4. International Commerce, Economics & Finance (2 slots)
  5. Humanities, Social Science & Fine Arts (4 slots)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Community**: Scheduled Tribe candidate.
2. **Age**: Below 35 years as on 1st July of the application year.
3. **Minimum Qualification Marks**:
   - For Post-Graduate: At least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Bachelor's Degree.
   - For Ph.D.: At least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Master's Degree.
4. **Income Ceiling**: Total family income from all sources must not exceed **Rs. 6,00,000 (Six Lakh Rupees per annum)**.
5. **Number of Children Restriction**: Not more than two children of the same parents/guardians can avail the scholarship.

## 4. Financial Benefits Provided Abroad
The scholarship covers complete international educational and living costs:

| Component | United States & Other Countries (USD) | United Kingdom (GBP) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Annual Maintenance Allowance** | USD 15,400 per annum | GBP 9,900 per annum |
| **Contingency Allowance** | USD 1,532 per annum | GBP 1,116 per annum |
| **Tuition & Examination Fees** | 100% Actual fees paid directly to the foreign university | 100% Actual fees paid directly to the foreign university |
| **Medical Insurance** | Actual premium covered | Actual premium / NHS surcharge covered |
| **Air Travel** | Economy class air passage by shortest route from India to host country & return upon completion | Economy class air passage by shortest route from India to host country & return upon completion |
| **Visa Fees** | Actual visa application fee reimbursed | Actual visa application fee reimbursed |

## 5. Tenure of Scholarship
- Master's Degree: 1 Year, 2 Years, or 3 Years (depending on course duration).
- Ph.D. Degree: Maximum 4 Years.
