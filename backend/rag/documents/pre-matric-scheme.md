# Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for ST Students (Class IX & X)
Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), Government of India

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
The Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Tribe (ST) students studying in Classes IX and X is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme aimed at:
- Supporting parents of ST children for education of their wards studying in Classes IX and X so that the incidence of drop-out, especially in the transition from the elementary to secondary stage, is minimized.
- Improving participation of ST children in Classes IX and X of the pre-matric stage, so that they perform better and have a better chance of progressing to the post-matric stage of education.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Group**: Student must belong to a Scheduled Tribe (ST) community officially recognized in the respective State/UT.
2. **Class of Study**: Must be enrolled in regular, full-time study in Class IX or Class X in a Government School, or in a School recognized by Govt. or a Central/State Board of Secondary Education.
3. **Income Ceiling**: Annual parental/guardian income from all sources must not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand per annum)**.
4. **Academic Condition**: Scholarship for studying in any class will be available for only one year. If a student has to repeat a class, she/he would not get scholarship for that class for a second (or subsequent) year.
5. **No Double Scholarship**: The student should not be holding any other scholarship awarded by the Central or State Government during the same academic year.

## 3. Financial Benefits & Rates
The scholarship provides financial assistance for 10 months in an academic year:

| Beneficiary Category | Monthly Maintenance Allowance | Duration | Total Annual Allowance | Books & Ad-hoc Grant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day Scholars** | Rs. 225 per month | 10 Months | Rs. 2,250 | Rs. 750 per annum |
| **Hostellers** | Rs. 525 per month | 10 Months | Rs. 5,250 | Rs. 1,000 per annum |

- **Additional Allowances for Students with Disabilities**:
  - Reader Allowance for Visually Handicapped: Rs. 160 per month.
  - Escort Allowance for Severely Disabled: Rs. 160 per month.
  - Special Pay for Teacher/Helper: Rs. 160 per month.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Caste Certificate**: Valid Scheduled Tribe Certificate issued by an authorized revenue officer (SDO/Tehsildar/BDO).
2. **Income Certificate**: Valid Income Certificate from competent authority certifying annual family income <= Rs. 2.5 Lakh.
3. **Previous Class Marksheet**: Marksheet of Class VIII (for Class IX) or Class IX (for Class X).
4. **School Bonafide & Enrollment ID**: Verification certificate signed by the Headmaster/Principal.
5. **Bank Account Details**: Active Aadhaar-seeded bank account in the name of the student or joint account with parent/guardian.
6. **Aadhaar Number / Enrollment Slip**: For biometric identity verification.

## 5. Application Steps on National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
1. **One-Time Registration (OTR)**: Register at `https://scholarships.gov.in` using Student Aadhaar and mobile number.
2. **Form Fill-up**: Select "Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for ST Students" under Ministry of Tribal Affairs.
3. **Document Upload**: Upload Caste, Income, and Bonafide certificates via DigiLocker integration or scanned PDF.
4. **School Verification (L1)**: Headmaster/INO verifies school attendance and enrollment.
5. **District Welfare Officer Verification (L2)**: DWO verifies caste & income parameters.
6. **DBT Disbursal**: Sanctioned funds transferred directly to Aadhaar-seeded bank account via PFMS/NPCI gateway.
