# Multilingual Tribal & Vernacular Support Architecture (Adi Vaani)
Aadi Vikas Portal • Inclusive Citizen Services

## 1. Supported Languages & Scripts
The ST Seva / Aadi Vikas Portal delivers conversational and informational assistance across 6 key constitutional and scheduled tribal languages:

1. **English (en)**: Official language for administrative documentation and pan-India institutional communication.
2. **हिंदी (hi)**: National official language widely spoken across Northern, Central, and Western tribal belts.
3. **संताली (sat / Ol Chiki & Devanagari)**: Spoken by the Santhal community across Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Bihar, and Assam. Features support for Ol Chiki script and Roman phonetic representation.
4. **मुंडारी (mun)**: Austroasiatic language spoken by the Munda community primarily in Jharkhand, Odisha, and West Bengal.
5. **भीली (bhili)**: Spoken by the Bhil indigenous community across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and Maharashtra.
6. **गोंडी (gondi)**: South-Central Dravidian language spoken by the Gond tribal people across Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

## 2. Adi Vaani Vernacular AI Platform
- **Voice-First Navigation**: Students in remote tribal habitations can tap the microphone to speak questions in their native dialect.
- **Phonetic & Acoustic Normalization**: Automatic transliteration allows input in standard Devanagari or Romanized tribal phonetics.
- **Localized Greetings & Prompts**:
  - English: "How can I assist you today?"
  - Hindi: "नमस्ते! मैं आपकी सहायता कैसे कर सकता हूँ?"
  - Santali: "जोहार! आज आम चेत् लेकाते मदद दाड़ेयामा?" (Johar! How can I assist you?)
  - Mundari: "जोहार! आयिं आमके चिलिकते गोड़ो दाड़ियामे?" (Johar! How can I assist you?)
  - Bhili: "राम राम! मु तमारी कांई मदद करी सकूं?" (Ram Ram! How can I help you?)
  - Gondi: "सेवा जोहार! नना मिकुन बोरंग सहायता किया परंतोन?" (Seva Johar! How can I assist you?)
